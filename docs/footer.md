# Footer

Public footer settings are edited at `/admin/settings/footer`.

Supported fields:

- Enabled/disabled
- Brand text
- Description
- Copyright text
- Support text
- Address
- Privacy Policy URL
- Terms URL
- Powered-by text

Footer URLs may be safe relative internal paths such as `/privacy` or absolute `http`/`https` URLs. Unsafe schemes such as `javascript:`, `data:`, and `file:` are rejected.

Known limit: structured social/navigation footer links are not yet modeled as separate rows.
